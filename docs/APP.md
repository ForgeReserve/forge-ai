# The apps

The gateway serves two web apps from `public/`: the Forge app at `/v2/`, which the Android and iPhone apps carry too, and the Classic chat page at `/`. Both are plain ES modules with no build step and load nothing from other origins. This page describes what each one does and where the code is. What they keep on the phone and what they send is in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). The Forge app's parts are modules: see the [module guide](modules/README.md).

## The Forge app (`/v2/`)

The plan it was built from is [V2-BUILD.md](V2-BUILD.md), and its screens are described in [design/SCREENS.md](design/SCREENS.md). It reuses the Classic page's helpers by import (`/js/files.js`, `/js/extract.js`, `/js/markdown.js`, `/js/qr.js`, `/js/scan.js`, `/js/lockkey.js`).

- **Home:** two rings. Gold is funds (full means the balance right after money last came in; with two balances, the total over both), green is memory (how much of the model's context is still free). Tapping either opens Funds and memory. Hold to talk sits in the middle; Type, Add (photo and file) and More sit below.
- **First run:** a phone that opens the home for the first time sees four short steps first, each one idea: icons first, a short heading and one short sentence, with the detail under Learn more on the same step. Welcome (no account, paid with eCash, private questions, history kept on the phone, and the invite code when the engine needs one), How you pay (the ways to add funds by icon, only those this engine offers and this phone can use: a paper note, free questions while the engine gives them out, a card in the Android app, and Lightning; under Learn more, what the two rings mean and that funds are lost with the phone), What it can and cannot hide (under Learn more, what Forge hides, and what it cannot: the network address unless Tor or a relay is used, the voice on its way through the engine, where money came from, what you type, a watched phone, being forced to unlock, wrong answers), and Private only (on by default; under Learn more, what it covers and what it does not; Turn it on if it was turned off). Every step can be skipped; Skip or Start marks it seen (`forge2.firstRun` in `localStorage`), so it shows once. An address that names another screen, or a photo shared in, opens that screen instead. More, How Forge works opens it again. Listen plays the step's heading and sentence from a clip recorded once, offline, in the app's own natural voice (Phonon 31m English, voice Maeve, British English; [VOICE.md](VOICE.md)), in `public/v2/voice/en/` (Opus in Ogg), which the service worker keeps for offline use. A clip plays only when its manifest says it was recorded from exactly the words the step shows; otherwise, or where it cannot play, Listen uses the phone's own voice where it has one that runs on the phone. The engine's voice is never used there (`public/v2/core/firstrun.js`, `public/v2/core/clips.js`, `public/v2/screens/firstrun.js`). After changing those words, record the clips again with `scripts/record-voice/record.py` (setup in its header; no key, no network service: Phonon runs in headless Chrome on this computer from the model files the build already has, with a fixed seed so the same words give the same sound; `--engine kokoro` still records with Kokoro's Emma); `test/unit/v2/voice-clips.test.ts` fails until you do.
- **Search the web:** for a model that can search (the private Tinfoil models), Type has a Search the web switch, and so does the home (for a question asked by voice) once there are funds. It is off by default and off again once a question is sent. While it is on, one line says that the sealed machine sends the search to a search service, which sees the search but not who asked; the price line includes the search fee; and the question carries `web_search_options` with both of Tinfoil's safety filters (`pii_check_options`, `prompt_injection_check_options`) inside the encrypted body, plus `X-Forge-Web-Search: 1` so the engine holds the fee. A search is never a free question.
- **Price before sending:** every request that costs money shows its price first: the Type screen, the line under the talk button while recording, Ask again and Try again on the answer screen, and Listen when the engine reads aloud. The price line reads Free, or Free, 2 of 3 left, when a free question pays. Choose a model dims a model the money on the phone cannot pay a short question to, with the Add money picture beside its name ("Add money first" to a screen reader), by the rules asking uses: a free model, a free question or an event pass's free balance is never marked, and with two balances the dollars pay first, then the sats with a rate (`core/balances.js` `cannotPay`). Choosing a marked model goes straight on to Add money in place of the list; once money is in, the success panel's Ask now goes home with that model chosen. The mark goes once money comes in.
- **Empty states:** with no funds, the home shows one Add money button under the rings (on a free model at $0: the model's name with FREE, and a small Add money button); Add money is the wallet cards, one picture card with one word per way ([design/SCREENS.md](design/SCREENS.md)); Funds and memory offers Add money as a fanned stack of those cards, with no Take out button while there is nothing to take out; History with nothing in it says what will be kept there and offers Ask a question.
- **Add money's screens:** each way's own screen has the picture of its card on Add money at the top (the two card ways show the printed card, and the tap while they wait for it), what a way holds or offers as a card with the amount in large type, the other buttons raised and one lit button at the bottom. When the money is in, one success panel says so: a check, the amount added, its words and the funds now, with Ask now as the next step and Done back to Funds. A way that goes back to Funds by itself (eCash, a note, a card, free questions) shows the same panel at the top of Funds, with Ask now under it. Take funds out has eCash's picture.
- **Hold to talk:** press and hold to record, let go to send, slide off the button and let go to cancel; a tap explains it, and Space or Enter work too. While it records, the button shows level bars, and a line under the rings gives the time, where the sound goes and the price before sending (turning the voice into words, the question, and reading the answer aloud when the engine does it). The recording goes straight to the answer, which shows the question as text and reads the answer aloud; Listen replays it and Stop stops it. Voice in and voice out are `voice-in` and `voice-out` modules ([module list](modules/list.md)). The browser recogniser may send the voice to the browser's maker, so it is never used with Private only on; with Private only on, voice goes only to an audio model in sealed hardware (tier `enclave`) or stays on the phone. It passes through the engine in plain form to get there, unlike a private question, which is encrypted end to end. Answers are read by the phone's own voice when it has one that runs on the phone, else by the engine; More, Voice changes either, or turns reading aloud off. Sound recorded on the phone stays in memory and is dropped once sent or cancelled; each piece of a spoken answer is kept in memory as soon as it arrives, for the last few answers, so Listen never pays twice for a piece, even after Stop part way or a refused play. The line under the button, the Voice screen and the answer screen while the engine reads aloud all say that the engine could hear or read what passes through it.
- **Engine client** (`public/v2/core/engine.js`): the OpenAI-compatible API ([API.md](API.md)), streamed. It answers a `428 pow_required` challenge once per request, the way a Bellows asks (`X-Bellows-Pow-Challenge`, solved into `X-Bellows-Pow`). This gateway does not send 428; the client is tested against a stub. Private models (enclave and encrypted in the browser) go through the vendored Tinfoil SDK, as in the Classic page.
- **Money first:** eCash is written to the phone (`forge2.vault` in localStorage) before it is sent and removed only when the engine confirms the credit. Taken-out eCash stays there until the person taps I saved it. The app uses the Classic page's balance key (`forge.balanceKey`), so a Classic balance carries over.
- **Two balances** ([PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md#two-balances), `public/v2/core/balances.js`): one in dollars and one in sats, each with its own key; the second key is kept in `forge2.balanceKey2` and made only when money in its unit first arrives, opened with the first key (`X-Forge-Sibling`), so no invite is asked. Money goes to the balance of its own unit with no change of units: sat eCash, notes and cards to sats, dollar ones to dollars, Lightning, a Fedimint card and on-chain to sats. Units are changed (price first) only on an engine that cannot open the second balance. A question is paid in full by the dollars when they cover its hold, else by the sats (with a BTC/USD rate), never split. The home shows one total: one balance in its own unit, both in dollars at the app's rate (the dollars alone without a rate). Funds lists both as rows, each with its own Take out, once both hold money.
- **Modules:** every swappable part has a manifest under `public/v2/modules/<kind>/<id>/` and loads through `public/v2/core/registry.js`, which refuses a bad manifest and hands a module only its kind's inputs. The [module list](modules/list.md) describes every bundled module. The registry does not yet check the `publisher` and `sig` fields and runs no sandbox: only the bundled modules load, as ordinary page code ([modules/README.md](modules/README.md#not-built)).
- **Paper notes:** Scan a note is the first way to add funds. The camera (through the camera adapter and the Classic page's scanner, so single and animated codes both work) reads the eCash on a paper note; the phone shows the amount and the mint before anything is sent, and adds it on one tap through the vault. A note from a mint this engine does not take says so and offers Other mint, with the note handed over. A note that was already used says so (the engine learns that from the mint when the note is added). With no funds, a chip right under the gold ring opens the scanner, and Funds and memory shows Scan a note first. If the camera cannot be used, the code can be pasted. [OPERATING.md](OPERATING.md#printing-paper-notes) shows how to print notes.
- **Share unseen (photos):** Add, Photo opens it; so does a photo shared in from another app (the manifest's `share_target`, caught by the v2 service worker). Take a photo or pick one, and the `filter` modules run on the phone, in order: `strip-details` lists what the file holds (where, which phone, when, a hidden thumbnail, other notes), `cover-faces` finds faces with the vendored face-api tiny face detector (MIT, on TensorFlow.js, model file bundled in `public/vendor/face-api/`) and `cover-numbers` finds lines of writing such as plates, signs and name tags with a small finder in plain JavaScript (`textfind.js`, no model). The person sees After and Before, adds a block with a tap or a drag, takes one away with a tap, and reads the list of what changed, including blocks taken away and any check that did not run. Share opens the phone's share sheet with the new file (a download where the browser has no sheet for files). Save puts it in Photos in the iPhone app, writes it where the person picks in the Android app, and downloads it on the website; the screen says Saved only for a save the phone confirmed, and on the website only that the download started. A save that was refused or failed says so, with what to do. Ask about it adds it to the next question (a paid question, with the price on the Type screen). In Choose a model, a model that can see photos (`capabilities.vision`) carries the photo badge among its tags: the photo picture alone, "Sees photos" to a screen reader, and "Sees photos. You can ask it about a photo." in its info line, which Listen reads with its name. When the model in use cannot see photos, Choose a model opens with only the models that can, under one line with the photo picture, the photo badge and the Add money mark as in the full list; the one picked takes the photo straight on to the Type screen. Private only still applies: with no private model that can see photos it says so, and Use Standard asks the Private chip's question first. With no model at all that can, a short message says so. Meanwhile the photo is held in the page's memory only (`app.js` `attachPhoto`, route `#/photo/model`). The new file is the photo drawn again with solid black blocks, widened to the JPEG encoder's 16 px grid so nothing under a block reaches the file, made into a new JPEG named `photo.jpg`, stripped of every APPn segment, comment and trailing data, and checked before it can leave (`public/v2/core/photo.js`, `photometa.js`). It needs no balance. The photo is held in the screen's memory only; the original is never stored or sent. The automatic checks can miss things (a face turned away, small or slanted writing) and the screen says so.
- **Private or Standard:** the chip at the top left of the home and the answer (both modes) shows the kind of model in use and switches it with one tap: back to the model of that kind chosen last on this phone (`lastPrivate`, `lastStandard` in the settings, kept by every choice of a model), else the cheapest Private model for a typical question, or the free model (else the cheapest) for Standard, turning Private only off on the way. The first time it leaves Private on a phone it asks Start free's question; a yes there or in Start free is kept (`standardOk`), a no changes nothing. A conversation on show stays on the screen and the next question starts a new one with the new model (`core/state.js` `kindSwitch`, `app.js` `togglePrivate`).
- **Free models** ([PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md), Free models): the model list shows a Free badge, the price line reads Free, the answer says "That was free.", and "not enough funds" never stops a question to one. **Start free:** an invite code alone opens a balance with nothing on it (`POST /v1/wallet/open`), from the first run's last step (its "Start free with an invite" link; answering the Private only question ends the first run) or from the Start free card, first on Add money while the engine has free models and the phone has no key or $0 on a paid model (its own screen: the code, Paste and Scan, then the question); a free model is then chosen if Private only allows one, and the free models work at $0. Paid models still need funds. With no balance key yet, Type says a free model needs a balance and opens Start free. The Classic page's model menu reads Free.
- **Event links** ([ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md), Event links): one QR code, `https://ai.forge-reserve.com/j#<pass>`, from an event pass (`pnpm admin passes:create`). On an iPhone or a computer `/j` opens the web app on `/v2/#join=<pass>`, which drops the pass from the address, opens a free balance with it, ends the first run and asks the Private only question once, then shows the home. On Android `/j` shows two steps (get the app, open it on the pass) and Use in browser; the app takes the pass through Android App Links or the page's intent link. A phone with a balance key already spends nothing.
- **Free questions** (off until the engine has a credit mint, [FREE-TIER.md](FREE-TIER.md)): Free questions is a tile on Add money's More card while the engine gives them out. The phone works the faucet's puzzle with a progress ring, and the voucher goes through the vault onto a **free balance** with its own key (`forge2.freeKey`), apart from the paid one. A voucher pasted as eCash or scanned as a note is counted in questions, never as an amount of money, and needs no invite. A free question is used first when the model is on the engine's free list and the question fits the caps: the price line reads "Free, 2 of 3 left", the answer says how many are left, the balance pill counts them when there is no paid balance, and the gold ring shows them as a lighter segment ahead of the paid balance. A voice question is never all free: the engine's speech to text is paid from the paid balance. When they run out, Type and the home lead to the three ways forward: more free questions, Scan a note, or Add money. The `/free` page gives a voucher as a QR code with Add to Forge (kept in the vault, then added when the app opens), Copy and Print.
- **Cashu cards** (Android app, and the iPhone app per [IOS.md](IOS.md); on the website the row says a page cannot read cards and points to `/download`; on an iPhone, to the iPhone app's link or how to put Forge on the Home Screen, [IOS.md](IOS.md), Getting the app): Tap a card reads the card over NFC (the app's ForgeNfc plugin), shows what it holds and its mint, asks for its PIN (a wrong one shows the tries left) and how much (all of it by default), and shows what arrives and what the card's mint takes. The card then signs one swap and the phone makes it at the card's mint through the engine (`/v1/wallet/fund/card/*`), unblinds its eCash and adds it like any eCash: a top-up for the engine's own mint, Other mint for another. Change goes back onto the card in the same tap. Before the card signs, everything needed to finish without it is stored on the phone (`forge2.cards`), so a tap that breaks loses nothing: the swap is sent again, the mint's signatures are asked for again (NUT-09), or the next tap of the same card gives its signature again. The protocol is ported from flash-pos and tested against the applet's own transcript and vectors, a simulated card, and the local mints ([PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md)). Not yet tried with a real card.
- **Fedimint cards** (Android app only): Tap a Fedimint card pays through a till (fedimintcard's `till serve --pay-enabled`), paired once by scanning its pairing code; the app keeps the till's token in the Android Keystore. With no till paired the screen first says the card needs a till and what a till does, then three numbered steps with pictures (get the pairing code from the person who runs the till, scan or paste it, then pay with the card); once paired it opens on "Till set." and what comes next. The iPhone app has no till part, so the way is not offered there and its address says Fedimint cards need the Android app for now. The engine makes a Lightning invoice, the screen shows the price before the tap (what reaches the funds, the most the card pays, and the till's part), the phone relays the till's card commands to the card over the till's card link, and the till charges the card and pays the invoice. A payment is stored on the phone before the till hears of it and is never charged twice; one the till could not pay is given back to the same card with Get it back ([PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md)). Tested against a mock till; not yet tried with a real till and card.
- **Storage:** IndexedDB `forge2` for settings, conversations and attachments, on this phone only. Start fresh clears the memory and starts a new conversation; the old one stays in History.
- **History, burn and auto-delete:** History (from More) lists past conversations, newest first, with their first words, time and, when auto-delete is timed, a bar with the time left. Tap one to open it and ask a follow-up to continue it; the flame burns it. Burn on the answer screen removes the open conversation at once; Burn all now removes every one and what waits for the next question. Auto-delete is Never, On close, 1 hour, 1 day (the default) or 1 week, applied when the app opens and by a timer while it is open (`public/v2/core/history.js`).
- **Erase everything** (from More): shows the balance that would be lost with Take funds out, warns about eCash not yet saved, and needs the button held for two seconds. It removes all of the site's `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB databases, caches and service workers (the Forge app and the Classic page share them), then checks that nothing is left (`public/v2/core/erase.js`). What each of these removes and what it cannot reach: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#the-v2-app-what-it-keeps-and-how-it-removes-it).
- **Service worker:** `public/v2/sw.js`, scope `/v2/`, network first with a cached copy for offline. It also catches a photo shared to `/v2/share` and keeps it in its own cache only until the page takes it. The Classic page's worker passes `/v2/` by. Only the worker's install creates its cache, so once Erase everything has removed it, nothing puts it back.
- **Strings:** every word is in `public/v2/strings/en.js`, the printed notes' words too.
- **Engine:** More, Server sets the address questions and payments go to (HTTPS only; the engine must answer before the app switches). The website's default is its own engine.
- **Modes:** Default (the rings) and Playful, with a cover game in front of the app ([PLAYFUL.md](PLAYFUL.md)).
- **Android app:** `android/` wraps the Forge app in Capacitor, with its files in the APK, application id `com.forgereserve.ai`, Android 9 and up, no Google services. It takes photos shared from other apps into Share unseen and shares out through Android's share sheet. `scripts/android-release.sh` signs a release, and `/download` offers the APK set in `FORGE_APK_PATH` with its SHA-256. A second APK, **Forge Private Voice** (`com.forgereserve.ai.privatevoice`, the `privatevoice` flavour, about 68 MB more), hears Hold to talk with its own speech to text on the phone (Moonshine tiny through sherpa-onnx), for GrapheneOS, which has no recogniser; `FORGE_APK_PRIVATE_VOICE_PATH` makes `/download` offer it beside the universal one. See [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md) and [VOICE.md](VOICE.md).
- **iPhone app:** `ios/` wraps the same bundle in Capacitor iOS, bundle id `com.forgereserve.ai`, iPhone only, iOS 16.4 and up. See [IOS.md](IOS.md) for what is native and what works as in Safari. Built and walked in the Simulator only.

## The landing page (`/about`)

`public/about/` is a static page that says what Forge AI is and who it is for, in the Forge app's icons and Midnight look (or the look the phone keeps from the app, through `public/v2/core/pagelook.js`). It is one screen with no scroll, from a 320 by 568 phone to a 1920 by 1080 screen: the title, one drawn phone in the app's two rings, and a row of four moments under the title (Talk, Private, Pay, Share unseen). `/about` moves to `/about/`, and the gateway serves it under its own Content-Security-Policy (`ABOUT_CSP` in `src/app.ts`): files from this origin only, no inline code, never framed. It loads nothing from another site. The Classic page's service worker does not keep it (it is not in the app shell), and the v2 worker's scope is `/v2/`.

- **Top right:** Open the app (`/v2/`), Download (a small panel, the browser's popover, with the Android and iPhone buttons and a link to the app in the browser) and the code on GitHub.
- **Download buttons:** each one's link is the `data-href` of its button in `public/about/index.html`. While that is empty, the button shows Soon and is no link (`public/about/about.js`).
- **The moments:** the phone moves through the app's screens (Talk shows the home and an answer, Private the Private only step, Pay the paper note's card and the cards' card, Share unseen a photo with its faces covered), each with one line of words and, where there is room, two labels beside the phone. Each moment lights a quarter more of the rings, as the first run does. The ring around the selected tab is a CSS animation and is the clock: its first half ending shows the moment's second screen, its second half ending moves on (`about.js`). Picking a tab, by tap or with the arrow keys, shows that moment and stops. Pause (the button after the tabs) or a tap on the phone stops it and starts it again; the mouse over the phone, the tabs or the words, keyboard focus on a tab, or the page out of sight holds it. Everything moves by transform and opacity only. Under reduced motion nothing moves: the rings are drawn whole, Pause is gone, and a tab changes the moment at once.
- **Phone pictures:** `public/about/shots/*.webp`, made by `pnpm about:shots` (`scripts/about-shots.ts`) from the v2 app on a local gateway with the local test mints (`pnpm mint:up`). The question and answer are written in the script. Run it again after a change to the screens it shows.
- **Tests:** `test/unit/about.test.ts` (headers, files, sizes, the moments' markup, words) and `test/ui/about.ui.test.ts` (one screen at six sizes with nothing outside the window and no layout shift, the actions, the moments moving on, picked and stopped, reduced motion, the download panel, Soon and a set link, no requests to other sites, no errors).

## The Classic chat page (`/`)

`/` serves a chat app from `public/`. It has a chat history sidebar kept in the browser's IndexedDB (search, rename, delete), streaming replies with a reasoning disclosure, Markdown with highlighted code and tables, stop, regenerate, edit and resend, copy, file attachments (documents for every model, images for models with `vision: true`), per-message cost and token counts, a model picker grouped by tier with price and speed hints (uncensored models behind an explicit opt-in), and Settings for theme, default model, reasoning effort, the system prompt and "Lock refunds to my wallet key" (a P2PK key kept in localStorage; refunds and other-mint change are then locked to it). The wallet drawer adds funds by eCash (paste or camera scan, including animated NUT-16 codes), Lightning, on-chain and eCash from other mints, and refunds as eCash with copy, a text-file download and a QR code (animated for long tokens). Keyboard shortcuts are listed under `Ctrl+/`.

**Files.** One attach button takes images (vision models) and documents (every model): text and code files, PDF and Word `.docx`. Files are read in the browser and only their text goes into the message, as `<file name="..." pages="...">...</file>` blocks before the typed text (at most 200,000 characters per message, cut with a note). Scanned PDF pages can be sent as images to vision models (up to 4, rendered on the device). Chips above the message box show name, size, pages and reading progress; the line under them estimates input tokens and cost and warns when the model's `context_length` would be exceeded. Drag and drop and paste work too. Extraction code (`public/js/extract.js`) and pdf.js load only when a document is attached; `.docx` is unzipped with the browser's `DecompressionStream`, so it needs no library.

**Installable app.** `public/manifest.webmanifest` (icons in `public/icons/`, built from the anvil mark by `scripts/icons.sh` with `rsvg-convert`; shortcuts `/#new` and `/#fund`), iOS home-screen meta tags, and a service worker. The gateway serves `/sw.js` from the template `public/sw.js`, filling in a version that is a SHA-256 of the app shell files (every file under `public/` the page needs: `index.html`, `app.js`, `js/*.js`, CSS, icons, manifest, offline page) and a second hash of `public/vendor` (`src/sw.ts`). It is computed when the gateway starts and again when a file changes, so a deploy needs no build step and every deploy that changes the page changes `/sw.js`. The worker precaches the shell as one set per version and serves it cache first, so new HTML is never paired with old JS; vendor files are cache first in their own versioned cache. A new version waits: the page shows "Update available" with Reload, and otherwise switches on the next launch. API calls, `/admin`, `/beta`, POSTs and requests with `Authorization` or `X-Cashu` bypass the worker. Offline, the app opens from the cache with the chat history, and sending is disabled until the connection returns; other pages fall back to `public/offline.html`. Settings offers "Install app" where the browser supports it, and on iOS a one-time Share > Add to Home Screen hint.

The attachment helpers load when the page is idle; the highlighter, QR, scanner, document extraction, pdf.js and Tinfoil SDK load on first use. Rebuild the vendored libraries with `scripts/vendor.sh`. Privacy details: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#chat-page).
