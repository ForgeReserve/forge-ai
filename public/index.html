Lock refunds to my wallet key

Refunds, and change or returned eCash from other-mint funding, are locked to this key (Cashu NUT-11 P2PK). Only the wallet holding the matching private key can redeem them, and the gateway cannot spend them. Find the key in cashu.me under Receive, then eCash, then the lock icon (or Settings, P2PK), shown as "P2PK Key" starting 02. Minibits and other nostr-based wallets lock to your npub. Leave it empty for ordinary tokens.

Use a key from a wallet you control. eCash locked to a key you do not hold is lost: nobody, including Forge, can unlock it.
